How to read this

  • UCI Verona labels each showtime with its audio and subtitle languages. The printed time is the admission time — the film itself starts about 25 minutes later (about 15 for alternative-content events).
  • The Cinema Fiume group (Kappadue, Diamante, Fiume, Pindemonte) marks original-version slots with an asterisk and subtitles are Italian, but the page never states the audio language. Audio here is inferred from the film's production country and director; each entry records how.
  • Showtimes with no original-version marker are Italian dubs and are left out.
  • Online ticketing exists only at UCI. The Fiume houses sell at the box office.
